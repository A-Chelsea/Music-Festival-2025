ICONIC LINEUP OF 2025

The biggest names in Afrobeats and Amapiano

Tyla

Amapiano / Pop

The global superstar bringing the heat from South Africa to the main stage. Fresh off her massive success with the Grammy-winning hit 'Water', Tyla is set to mesmerise the crowd with her signature pop-amapiano fusion and electric dance moves. A performance you simply cannot miss.

Tems

Afrobeats|R&B

A defining voice of her generation, the Grammy-winning singer-songwriter and producer is known for her distinctive, soul-stirring vocals. Blending alternative R&B with Afrobeats, Tems delivers a live experience that is as emotional as it is powerful.

Ayra Starr

Afrobeats|R&B

The 'Sabi Girl' herself is touching down at Music Fest. Since exploding onto the scene with her viral anthem 'Bloody Samaritan', Ayra has captivated the world with her unique R&B-Afrobeat sound and unapologetic energy. Get ready for a set full of vibes and attitude.

Uncle Waffles

Afrobeats

The Princess of Amapiano returns! Hailing from Eswatini, this globally recognised DJ, producer, and dancer knows exactly how to control a crowd. With hits like 'Tanzania' and 'Yahyuppiyah', she guarantees a high-energy set that will keep the Amapiano Dome shaking all night.

Rema

Afrobeats

The Rave Lorde is here. A Grammy-winning icon and the force behind the global smash 'Calm Down', Rema has taken Afrobeats to new heights. From 'Charm' to 'Azaman', expect a hit-filled performance that showcases why he is one of the biggest names in music today.

Asake

Afrobeats

Mr. Money with the vibe! As one of the most streamed artists of the year, Asake brings his infectious blend of Fuji, Street Pop, and Amapiano to the festival. With anthems like 'Mr. Money' and 'Lady', his stage presence is legendary; expect chaos in the best way possible.
